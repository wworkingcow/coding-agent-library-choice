Email and password sign-up and sign-in, Google sign-in, and a protected `/dashboard` are all in place. `next build` passes. Signed-out requests to `/dashboard` get a 307 redirect to `/signin`, and `/signin` loads. I did not test sign-up, sign-in or Google login end to end, so none of those have run. Google also can't work until you add real credentials.  I used Auth.js (`next-auth@5` beta), with `zod` for input validation. It fits the Next.js App Router, and Google plus email/password is built in. Passwords are hashed with Node's built-in scrypt, so there are no native dependencies.  - **`src/
