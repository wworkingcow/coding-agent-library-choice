Notely now has email + password sign up and sign in, Google sign-in, and a protected `/dashboard`. I tested it against a production build: sign up, a duplicate email, a wrong password, sign in, the dashboard with a valid session, sign out, a fake session cookie, and the redirect to Google. All behaved correctly, and type-check and lint pass. The Google redirect only produces a valid-looking URL with the placeholder client ID. A real Google login hasn't been run, because that needs real credentials.  **What I chose** - **[Better Auth](https://better-auth.com)** handles email/password and Google
