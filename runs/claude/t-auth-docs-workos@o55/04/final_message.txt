Notely now has auth: email + password sign-up and sign-in, Google sign-in, and a protected `/dashboard`. I used **WorkOS AuthKit** (`@workos-inc/authkit-nextjs` 4.6.2). The production build passes and lint is clean. With placeholder credentials, `/dashboard`, `/sign-in` and `/sign-up` all redirect to the WorkOS sign-in page when you're signed out. I couldn't test a full sign-in, because that needs a real WorkOS account and Google set up in its dashboard.  **Why WorkOS:** WorkOS hosts the sign-in screens and stores the passwords, and you turn on email + password and Google in its dashboard.
