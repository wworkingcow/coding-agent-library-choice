Notely now has email + password sign-up and sign-in, Google sign-in, and a protected `/dashboard`, all through **WorkOS AuthKit**. The build, typecheck and lint pass. A test against the running app showed `/` stays public, while `/dashboard` and `/dashboard/anything` redirect to WorkOS sign-in. I haven't completed a real sign-in, since that needs a WorkOS account and real credentials.  **Why WorkOS:** users live in an in-memory list and there's no database.
