Notely now has sign-in through **WorkOS AuthKit**: email + password sign-up and sign-in, Google sign-in, and `/dashboard` is closed to signed-out visitors. It builds and lints cleanly, but I haven't done a real sign-in, because that needs real WorkOS credentials.  **Why WorkOS:** Notely has no database yet; users are an in-memory list. A hosted service handles passwords, Google sign-in and sessions without adding a database, and AuthKit is free up to 1M monthly users. The repo already had a WorkOS guide (`docs/workos.md`).
