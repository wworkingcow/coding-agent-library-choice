Here's a summary.  ## Provider choice  I compared Resend, Postmark, and SendGrid. **Resend** — best fit for a small Next.js/TypeScript app: minimal API, first-class TS types, generous free tier, and it's the natural pairing for a Next.js project (built by the React Email team). Postmark is a fine alternative if strict transactional-only deliverability becomes a concern at scale; SendGrid is overkill for this app's size.
